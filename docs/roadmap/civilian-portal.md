A separate, public tablet that uses the same records database.

## Character dashboard
Profile, personal information, registered vehicles, driver's / firearm / pilot license status (with the points bar), active warrants, citations, arrests and medical history.

## 911
* Choose the call type: **Police, Fire, Medical or DOT**.
* The character's name and phone number are attached automatically.
* Give the location and describe the emergency.
* The call goes to whichever department receives that call type (set in the Admin Dashboard).
* **Track the call live:** Received → Unit assigned → En route → On scene → Closed, with updates from dispatch.

## Personal records
Citations, arrests, warrants, licenses, registered vehicles, business registrations and submitted service requests.

## Applications
Driver's, firearm and pilot licenses, business registration, vehicle registration and license renewal, with automatic checks. For example:
* a firearm license is denied while the person has an active warrant
* a driver's license is denied while it's suspended

## Service requests
Roadside assistance, road hazard reports, non-emergency assistance and public works requests. Each goes to the right department's board.
