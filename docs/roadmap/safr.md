Preview mockup. It isn't in the game yet.

## Dispatch board
* Live SAFR apparatus board with firefighter and EMS unit statuses: **Available, En Route, On Scene, Transporting, At Hospital, Busy, Out of Service**.
* Active medical and fire calls, color-coded by priority.
* Assign Self, En Route, On Scene, Transporting, Clear.
* Create calls by hand, and receive civilian 911 medical and fire calls.

## Incident records
* Call type: **Medical, Fire, Rescue, MVC, Hazmat**.
* Priority, location, caller and callback number.
* Apparatus and personnel assigned, and the patient count.
* Timestamped notes and status changes, incident history and nearby call history.
* Linking to LEO and DOT on the same incident.

## Patient records
Name and character ID, injury, severity, treatment given, where they were taken, condition, and each person's full medical history.

## Reports
Patient Care Report, Fire Incident, Rescue, Vehicle Collision, Hazardous Material and Hospital Transport. Narratives need at least 20 characters.

## Alerts
Active medical calls, active fire and hazmat calls, rescue requests, mutual aid requests, **Mayday** (flashing, with a PAR clear), and station-wide announcements.

## Management
Fire stations, apparatus and vehicles (including putting them out of service), callsigns, ranks, a certifications grid (FF I/II, EMT, Paramedic, Driver/Operator, Hazmat Ops, Rescue Tech) and the personnel roster.
