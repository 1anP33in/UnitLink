This one feels more like a **work order and roadside operations system** than a police CAD.

## Job board
* Live DOT units. Statuses: **Available, En Route, On Scene, Working, Busy, Unavailable**.
* Active work orders, with filters for roadside assistance, traffic control, vehicle recovery, maintenance and construction.
* Create jobs by hand. Civilian service requests arrive on the board automatically.

## Work orders
Work order number, job type, description, priority, location, assigned employees, required equipment (pulled from inventory), job status (Open, Assigned, In Progress, On Hold), completion notes, and the time opened and closed.

## Job types
Road maintenance, roadside assistance, vehicle recovery, road closures, traffic management, road inspections, construction zones, debris removal, damaged road signs and highway hazards.

## Fleet & equipment
* **Vehicles assigned to employees:** tow trucks, service trucks and road maintenance vehicles, with their availability.
* **Equipment inventory:** check items out and back in.
* **Maintenance records:** a damage report can take a vehicle out of service.

## Reports
Road hazard, vehicle recovery, road closure request (approved by a supervisor), road inspection, maintenance completion and equipment damage.

## Road alerts
Road closures, major highway hazards (sent to every department), traffic control requests, emergency roadside requests and active construction zones.
