# FAQ

<details>
<summary>Are there fines?</summary>

No. Citations add license points. At 12 points, the driver's license is suspended.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a regular officer make a warrant active?</summary>

No. It waits for a supervisor's approval. A supervisor's own warrant goes active right away. Admins can change this with `Config.WarrantsNeedApproval`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Fire / EMS use UnitLink?</summary>

Not yet. By default only LEO can open it. A Fire & Rescue CAD is on the [roadmap](../roadmap/safr.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do licenses come from?</summary>

UnitLink-Core character metadata, key `licenses`. A future DMV script only has to write that key.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does UnitLink work without UnitLink-UI?</summary>

Yes. You just won't see its notifications.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change the command, key or laws?</summary>

Yes. See [UnitLink Config](../configuration/unitlink-config.md) and [Editing the Laws](../configuration/laws.md).
</details>
