# Alerts, Warrants & BOLOs

The **Alerts** screen has three sections.

## Warrants waiting for approval

Only supervisors see this section. Each warrant shows the subject, charges, probable cause and who requested it.
* **Approve:** the warrant goes active, and the subject shows **WANTED** in state returns.
* **Deny:** the warrant is closed.

## Active warrants

Every active warrant. **Mark Served** once the person is in custody.

## BOLOs and alerts

**+ Add Alert** creates a BOLO. Types:
* BOLO, Caution, Armed & Dangerous, Officer Safety, Missing Person, Stolen Vehicle

An alert can be linked to a person (add it from their state return), and it can have a plate. Every on-duty unit is notified. **Remove** takes it down.
