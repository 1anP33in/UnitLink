# Call Records

Double-click any call to open its record.

## Left side

* **Units** attached.
* **Created on** and **Source:** 911, or CAD for self-initiated calls.
* **Nature** and **Priority:** editable. Each change adds a system note, and the 911 card updates too.
* **Address, City, County, Caller, Primary Unit.**
* **Buttons:**
  * **Assign Self**, when you're not attached.
  * **ENRT**, **ARRVD** and **Clear Self**, when you are.
* **Top-right icons:** **GPS** (sets a waypoint) and **Close call**.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Notes** | Live, timestamped notes. System entries are added for attaches, status changes and edits, and notes from other units appear right away. |
| **Info** | Call details and the 911 caller's statement |
| **State Returns** | Names run while attached to this call |
| **Address History** | Closed calls within 60 m of this address |

## New Call (self-initiated)

**New Call** or **+**:
1. Choose the nature and priority, and add notes.
2. The call is created at your position, and you're attached automatically.
3. Other units **don't** get a popup.

## Closing a call

Close it with the trash icon. Then:
* It moves to [Call History](laws-and-history.md).
* Its units go back to **AVAIL**.
* Reopening it shows it **read-only**.
