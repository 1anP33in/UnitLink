# Forms & Reports

| Form | Needs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Citation** | A subject and at least 1 charge | No felonies. Adds **license points**, plus a **fine on record only** (nothing charged, expires) |
| **Arrest Report** | A subject, at least 1 charge, a narrative of 20+ characters | Choose where they're booked |
| **Incident Report** | A narrative of 20+ characters | The subject is optional |
| **Warrant Request** | A subject, probable cause of 20+ characters | Needs a supervisor's approval: see [Warrants](../rules/warrants.md) |

## Adding charges

Type in the charge box. It searches by:
* **code:** `2-01`
* **name:** `speeding`
* **abbreviation:** `dui`, `gta`, `pwid`, `dws`

Click a result to add it. The table shows each charge's class (Felony, Misdemeanor, Infraction) and its points.

## Linking a call

Choose the call under **Linked Call**. A system note is added to that call when you file the form.

## My Records

The panel on the right lists everything you've filed. Click one to read it back.
